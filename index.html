The central question

What would it take to make a good life possible for everyone?

Not merely to tell individuals to live better, but to understand and shape the social, economic, cultural, ecological and personal conditions that make flourishing more or less possible.

The architecture

Two lenses.
One connected reality.

One examines the conditions we live within. The other examines the lives those conditions should enable. Their integration turns understanding into design.
