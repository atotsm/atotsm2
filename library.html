# The Sovereign Library *A guided index of the ideas, inquiries, instruments, experiments, and creative work gathered around ATOTSM.* The Library is an evolving public knowledge space—not a claim that every listed idea is complete, validated, or published. Its purpose is to make the work easier to discover, inspect, question, and develop. ## How to read this Library Each entry should carry a maturity label. These labels describe the state of the work, not its importance. - **Concept** — an idea, question, or proposed framework. - **In development** — active drafting, design, or implementation is underway. - **Artifact available** — a tangible document, model, codebase, instrument, or media item can be inspected. - **Tested** — a defined test or evaluation has been performed; its method and result should be linked. - **Published** — a public, inspectable version is available. - **Evidence-backed** — specific claims are supported by traceable evidence, with scope and limitations stated. - **Unverified here** — mentioned in the wider project inventory, but its underlying artifact or evidence has not yet been located in this repository audit. A work may have more than one label. “Published” does not automatically mean “tested” or “evidence-backed.” ## Explore by territory ### 1. 2. **Claim:** What does it propose, and what does it not claim? 3. **Method:** How was it developed, calculated, tested, or interpreted? 4. **Inputs and assumptions:** What choices materially affect the result? 5. **Artifact:** Where can a visitor inspect, run, read, or play with it? 6. **Result:** What did it produce, and what was observed? 7. **Limitations and counterarguments:** Where might it fail or mislead? 8. **Revision history:** What changed, why, and what remains open? 9. **Invitation:** How can a visitor question, reproduce, or contribute without implied obligation? ## Roadmap 1. **M0 — Evidence audit:** inventory public pages and known artifacts; label maturity and uncertainty. 2. **M1 — Library foundation:** publish this navigable map and conservative artifact register. 3. **M2 — Flagship artifact 1:** select the strongest real artifact and publish it to the standard above. 4. **M3 — Flagship artifacts 2–3:** bring two further artifacts to the same inspectable standard. 5. **M4 — Connected worked example:** connect systemic diagnosis → human consequences → alternative design → practical experiment → reflection. 6. **M5 — Laboratory experience:** make one real tool, simulation, or prototype usable by visitors. 7. **M6 — Participation pathways:** clarify the Audit, Hearth, and Open Encounter invitations and boundaries. 8. **M7 — Quality review:** accessibility, mobile usability, navigation, evidence, and editorial review. ### M1 acceptance criteria - The Library has a clear entry point and explains its purpose. - Concepts, artifacts, tests, publication, and evidence are not conflated. - Unlocated work is marked honestly rather than silently omitted or described as nonexistent. - Visitors can identify a next step: explore, inspect an artifact, question a claim, or participate. - No claim of validation, impact, or readiness exceeds the evidence linked. --- *This index is a first-pass map based on the public ATOTSM site and the project inventory available during the review.
