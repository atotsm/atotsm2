The organising question

What can greater awareness make possible?

How can we become more conscious of the systems shaping our lives, recover agency, discover what helps people flourish, and consciously shape the systems we live within?

How to use this map. The short descriptions are the overview. Select “Unfold the territory” to reveal its internal structure, questions and examples. Follow links when you want to enter an existing part of the website.
Six connected territories

Choose a door. Follow the connections.

These are not six separate programmes or a compulsory sequence. They are different ways into one evolving inquiry.

01 · SEESystemic awarenessInvestigate the monetary and wider structures shaping the conditions in which people live.Unfold the territory ＋

Usurology and the conditions around us

Usurology is the proposed diagnostic inquiry into usury, monetary arrangements, ownership, institutions and their interacting effects. A central concern is persistent usury unawareness: what remains difficult to recognise, how it shapes choices, and how its consequences are distributed.

  • Usurpia and the wider systemic landscape
  • Persistent Usury Unawareness and the PPPPP lens
  • Credit, debt, ownership, power and institutional dynamics
  • Effects across personal, relational, communal, societal, ecological and other dimensions

02 · UNDERSTANDInvestigation and evidenceMake questions inspectable through first principles, models, historical inquiry, evidence and revision.Unfold the territory ＋

From interpretation to challengeable inquiry

The investigative method connects claims to mechanisms, sources, counter-evidence, uncertainty and tests. Models are tools to examine—not conclusions visitors must accept.

  • The Lens, first-principles reasoning and the Paradigms
  • Historical, institutional, linguistic and theological inquiry
  • Simulations such as the Three Villages and the Wörgl exploration
  • Claim registers, falsifiability, evidence limits and revision
Explore the method →
03 · RECONNECTHuman flourishingExplore needs, vitality, belonging, agency, purpose, capabilities and meaningful contribution.Unfold the territory ＋

Ikigaiology and the human dimension

Ikigaiology is the constructive counterpart: an inquiry into what helps people and communities flourish. It connects personal development with material conditions, relationships, freedom, care and the living world—without turning purpose into another productivity demand.

  • Needs, health, care, belonging and agency
  • Signature strengths, learning, meaning and mastery
  • Personal, relational, communal and ecological flourishing
  • Soevereign inquiry and practices for reconnecting with capacity
Explore Ikigaiology →
04 · REIMAGINEMonetary and social alternativesExplore voluntary monetary pluralism, currency democracy, commons and cooperative governance.Unfold the territory ＋

Plurality rather than another monopoly

Currency democracy asks how people can meaningfully choose and combine monetary arrangements. Different systems may serve different purposes; each needs appropriate access, rights, governance, transparency, safeguards and a fair way to leave.

  • National, local, mutual-credit, time-based and purpose currencies
  • Currency Democracy and complementary-currency creation
  • Commons, cooperatives, community governance and digital infrastructure
  • Plural systems designed around real needs, not tokens for their own sake
Enter Currency Ecology →
05 · EXPERIMENTPractical creationTurn ideas into games, prototypes, tools, community practices and bounded experiments.Unfold the territory ＋

The Sovereign Laboratory

The Laboratory is the applied side: learning through play, testing currency designs, building shared infrastructure and exploring material resilience. A project name in this map indicates an area of work or exploration, not by itself a completed or validated result.

  • Pedagogy: Polypoly, Top Trumps and the Playful Economic Playbook
  • Currency concepts: Bread Token, Festicoin, Bloom, 3-in-1 and Community Recovery Note
  • Digital commons: Unyt Smart Agreements and Yutho
  • Ecological and practical grounding: gardening, local resources and community experiments
Explore the current laboratory page →
06 · ENVISIONCivilisational possibilitiesAsk what more conscious, plural and flourishing forms of collective life could become.Unfold the territory ＋

From awareness towards Jubilee

This horizon gathers constitutional, cultural, ethical and institutional questions. It is an invitation to imagine and examine possible futures—not a claim that one blueprint can resolve every context.

  • Jubilee strategies and managed transitions
  • Post-Usurpian constitutional and governance proposals
  • Intergenerational responsibility and ecological continuity
  • Culture, stories, music, play and shared meaning

Three ways to meet

Different invitations. One human conversation.

People may approach the inquiry in different ways. These are modes of encounter, not professional personas or identities anyone has to adopt.

01 · THE AUDIT

Investigate together

For people and groups interested in systemic questions, evidence, policy, community design and the relationship between monetary arrangements and lived outcomes.

02 · THE HEARTH

Meet through everyday life

For walks, gardens, thoughtful conversations and gift-based connection—meeting as people, not reducing one another to professional or client roles.

03 · THE OPEN ENCOUNTER

Begin without a script

For an unprescribed exchange: curiosity, integrity and the possibility of an interesting meeting without a predetermined task or expected outcome.

A principle for the whole website

Depth when curiosity asks for it.

Start with a clear snapshot. Reveal definitions, examples, sources, methods and project specifics when a visitor chooses to explore further. Essential context and qualifications should remain visible; detail should be available without becoming an obstacle.

Respectful disclosure. Present the smallest useful amount of information first. Reveal greater depth, structure and substantiation when curiosity invites it. Keep every layer navigable, accessible and connected to the wider map.
