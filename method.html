The method · The proof room

Ideas earn trust
through scrutiny.

ATOTSM is an evolving framework. Its claims should be inspectable, assumptions visible, predictions testable and conclusions revisable. The purpose of scrutiny is to improve the map, not defend an identity.

Claim discipline

Do not blur different kinds of knowledge.

Every substantive statement should make clear what kind of statement it is and what would count as support or challenge. This does not mean treating every issue as equally uncertain: exploitative usury is a documented historical and contemporary problem. The scrutiny concerns the particular mechanism, scope, scale, causal pathway and proposed remedy in each claim.

01

Observation

A recorded event, measurement, document or first-person account. State how obtained and what it cannot establish.

02

Evidence

Material bearing on a question. Assess quality, scope, uncertainty and alternatives.

03

Interpretation

An explanation of observations with assumptions and causal steps explicit.

04

Hypothesis

A proposition that generates testable expectations and could be weakened or rejected.

05

Proposal

A suggested design or intervention evaluated for feasibility, distribution and risk.

06

Finding

A conclusion supported by a stated method and bounded evidence.

Carry forward the original proof room

Core questions — with room for counterevidence.

The earlier site raised important propositions. Here they are reframed as research questions rather than settled conclusions.

Q1

Debt, autonomy and precarity

Under what conditions are higher household or public debt burdens associated with lower perceived autonomy, greater financial stress or reduced capacity to absorb shocks?

Q2

Local exchange and resilience

Do communities using well-governed complementary exchange systems retain more useful activity during external disruptions than comparable communities?

Q3

Systemic understanding and agency

Does distinguishing personal, organisational and structural causes improve decision quality or perceived agency?

Q4

Work, meaning and wellbeing

How do autonomy, purpose, workload, security and recognition interact in meaningful work?

A worked example · tracing costs

What exactly is inside the price?

Margrit Kennedy’s Interest and Inflation Free Money argues that costs associated with capital and interest can be passed through into prices and fees. In the discussion around Figure 3, she gives examples of 12% for garbage collection, 38% for drinking water and 77% for social housing. Crucially, the accompanying text calls these the capital share of costs. Capital costs are not automatically identical to interest paid. A modern audit should trace the actual financing component, fees, margins and taxes at each supply-chain stage, then identify who ultimately bears them. This extends Kennedy’s question; it does not claim her book measured every stage of every supply chain. Read the source (PDF) →

Falsifiability

What would make us revise the framework?

These are starting points for operational definitions and research design, not results already established.

F1

Monetary mechanism

If robust models and real-world comparisons do not show the predicted effects under stated conditions, narrow or reject that claim.

F2

Autonomy prediction

If well-designed studies repeatedly find no meaningful relationship after accounting for plausible confounders, revise the hypothesis.

F3

Complementary currency

If a pilot creates more burden, exclusion or instability than useful exchange, do not scale it.

F4

Purpose intervention

If an intervention increases pressure or unpaid labour without improving chosen outcomes, redesign or discontinue it.

Important: a failed pilot does not automatically falsify an entire field. It may falsify a design, mechanism or prediction. State the scope of every conclusion.
A repeatable protocol

Make each investigation challengeable.

Define the question, pre-commit predictions, document evidence and publish results — including null results and unintended effects.

01

Define

State terms, population, mechanism and outcome.

02

Pre-commit

Record predictions, measures and failure conditions.

03

Collect

Document sources, consent, limits and uncertainty.

04

Review

Publish results, counterarguments and revisions.

A constitutional principle for inquiry

No claim is above revision.

The framework should never require loyalty to a founder, a label or a conclusion. Authority comes from reasons that can be inspected, tested and challenged. The people affected by a proposal must have a voice in evaluating it.

If a map cannot show where it is uncertain, where it may be wrong, and how it can be corrected, it is not yet a trustworthy map.
