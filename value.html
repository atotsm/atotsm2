Economic sustainability · Quiet contribution

Support the work.
Empower the doers.

ATOTSM exists to deepen awareness and understanding, especially around usury and the monetary structures shaping everyday life. The aim is to inspire the people who will teach, organise, build and develop alternatives—not to place one public personality at the centre of their work.

A sustainable model should protect open knowledge, support careful inquiry and make discreet assistance possible while leaving initiative, ownership and public leadership with the people doing the work.

The reason this belongs here

A project about economic conditions must examine its own.

Good research and useful projects take time, skill and resources. ATOTSM aims to keep essential awareness and inquiry accessible, while making room for deeper private assistance, carefully prepared research and support for people developing practical alternatives.

The work can be influential without being personality-led. Its value is measured less by the visibility of its originator than by the understanding, independence and capability it helps others develop.

Keep essential understanding open. Build things people genuinely value. Let the value created help sustain the work.
A layered value model

Open awareness. Deeper inquiry. Quiet assistance.

Different layers serve different purposes. Public understanding should travel freely; private work can support people who need depth, perspective or discreet help.

01 · Public

Open knowledge

Public introductions, core concepts, research notes, selected resources and the right to question or disagree. The basic intellectual entry point should remain accessible.

02 · Products

Deeper materials

Books, carefully researched dossiers, practical guides, workbooks, maps, toolkits and structured learning experiences that save people time or help them act.

03 · Membership

Ongoing participation

Optional membership for deeper research, working sessions, early access, discussion and involvement in developing projects. Membership should add value, not purchase a voice or grant authority over truth.

04 · Private

Discreet assistance

Confidential philosophical conversations, systemic perspective, research support, thoughtful introductions and behind-the-scenes guidance for people carrying ideas into practice.

05 · Enablement

Equip the doers

Help educators, thinkers, organisers and community builders connect insights, test assumptions and strengthen their own work. They retain ownership and public agency.

06 · Collaboration

Support without taking over

Contribute selectively to initiatives when invited and when the purpose is clear. Complementary-currency design may follow awareness and understanding, but should never be detached from them.

Two connected ambitions

Inspire the inspirers. Leave room for them to lead.

ATOTSM contributes understanding and perspective. Other people and communities translate that understanding into education, organisation, currency practice and practical change.

◌
The quiet contribution

Sustain the inquiry

Support open research, careful explanations, accessible resources and the infrastructure needed to keep the inquiry independent, useful and continuous.

✳
The wider ecosystem

Enable independent action

Help people recognise patterns, develop their own understanding, connect with others and carry ideas into their communities. They are not spokespeople for ATOTSM; they are independent actors in their own right.

The rules that make it coherent

Monetisation without betraying the mission.

Revenue is a means of sustaining useful work, not proof that the work is valuable or permission to manipulate people.

Genuine value first

Charge for work that provides clear value: insight, time saved, capability, access, coordination or a useful result. Never manufacture anxiety to create demand.

Transparency

Disclose sponsorships, affiliate income, commissions, conflicts of interest, pricing logic and how shared revenue is allocated.

Access and dignity

Keep essential public knowledge accessible where possible. Avoid making participation dependent on wealth, unpaid labour or constant availability.

Fair contribution

Recognise research, creation, care, moderation, maintenance and coordination. Agree on ownership, credit and compensation before value is commercialised.

Voluntary exchange

People should understand what they are buying or joining, be free to decline, and have clear terms for cancellation, refunds and exit.

Plural routes

Use ordinary money, memberships, cooperative ownership, mutual exchange, grants or sponsorship where each genuinely fits. No single revenue model is universal.

Build in a sensible order

Awareness first. Capability next. Practice where it belongs.

ATOTSM follows an awareness-led sequence. Practical implementation belongs with the people and communities who choose to carry it forward.

01

Awaken awareness

Make usury and its possible systemic effects easier to recognise and investigate.

02

Deepen understanding

Connect monetary structures with lived experience, institutions, relationships and human flourishing.

03

Reveal alternatives

Help people understand complementary currencies and other forms of cooperation, including their trade-offs and safeguards.

04

Enable others

Offer discreet assistance where useful, then leave ownership, leadership and implementation with the people doing the work.

Guiding principle: complementary-currency design is a possible downstream collaboration, not the entry product. The starting point is awareness of usury and the understanding needed to judge whether an alternative is appropriate.
Still to be decided

The model should evolve with evidence.

How can usury awareness reach people who can carry it further? Which explanations genuinely deepen understanding? What should always remain open? How can private assistance remain discreet, accountable and sustainable? Which independent initiatives could benefit from behind-the-scenes support?

These questions should be answered through small tests, transparent decisions and feedback — not by adopting a fashionable business model.

The goal is not to build a personality cult or make every initiative depend on ATOTSM. It is to sustain the inquiry while helping other people become more capable of independent thought and action.
