Economic sustainability · Shared value

Create value.
Share value.

ATOTSM investigates why our world works as it does—and develops ideas, tools and real-world alternatives. To keep that work moving, we need people who see its value: readers, collaborators, clients and future partners who want to help turn insight into practical change.

The ambition is twofold: make ATOTSM itself economically sustainable, and help other people discover, develop, exchange and earn from the value they create.

The reason this belongs here

A project about economic conditions must examine its own.

Good research and useful projects take time, skill and resources. ATOTSM therefore aims to offer things people can actually use—clear explanations, research, practical guides, workshops, tools and collaborative projects. Some knowledge stays openly accessible; paid work helps fund deeper development, fair contributor compensation and new experiments.

The challenge is to create a healthy relationship between usefulness, accessibility, contribution and income.

Keep essential understanding open. Build things people genuinely value. Let the value created help sustain the work.
A layered value model

Open foundations. Earned depth. Shared opportunity.

Not every part of the project needs the same economic model. Different forms of value can support one another.

01 · Public

Open knowledge

Public introductions, core concepts, research notes, selected resources and the right to question or disagree. The basic intellectual entry point should remain accessible.

02 · Products

Deeper materials

Books, carefully researched dossiers, practical guides, workbooks, maps, toolkits and structured learning experiences that save people time or help them act.

03 · Membership

Ongoing participation

Optional membership for deeper research, working sessions, early access, discussion and involvement in developing projects. Membership should add value, not purchase a voice or grant authority over truth.

04 · Services

Applied expertise

Research, workshops, facilitation, systems mapping, opportunity analysis, learning design and support for organisations or communities exploring alternatives.

05 · Ventures

Practical creations

Tools, platforms, publications, experiments and initiatives that emerge from the laboratory. Some may become independent projects or cooperative enterprises.

06 · Exchange

Shared opportunities

Help people find collaborators, audiences, resources and routes to market. Where ATOTSM helps create or coordinate measurable value, a transparent fee or agreed share may be appropriate.

Two connected ambitions

Support the platform. Enable the participants.

These are not competing purposes. A healthy platform can create infrastructure and opportunities that help others flourish; successful participants can, in turn, strengthen the ecosystem.

◌
The project itself

Sustain ATOTSM

Earn income from useful publications, memberships, services, tools and ventures. Reinvest in research, quality, infrastructure, accessibility and continuity. Pay contributors fairly as the project becomes able to do so.

✳
The wider ecosystem

Enable others

Help people recognise their capabilities, connect with others, test ideas, build products or services, reach audiences and participate in the value they help create. Not everyone needs to become an entrepreneur; meaningful contribution has many forms.

The rules that make it coherent

Monetisation without betraying the mission.

Revenue is a means of sustaining useful work, not proof that the work is valuable or permission to manipulate people.

Genuine value first

Charge for work that provides clear value: insight, time saved, capability, access, coordination or a useful result. Never manufacture anxiety to create demand.

Transparency

Disclose sponsorships, affiliate income, commissions, conflicts of interest, pricing logic and how shared revenue is allocated.

Access and dignity

Keep essential public knowledge accessible where possible. Avoid making participation dependent on wealth, unpaid labour or constant availability.

Fair contribution

Recognise research, creation, care, moderation, maintenance and coordination. Agree on ownership, credit and compensation before value is commercialised.

Voluntary exchange

People should understand what they are buying or joining, be free to decline, and have clear terms for cancellation, refunds and exit.

Plural routes

Use ordinary money, memberships, cooperative ownership, mutual exchange, grants or sponsorship where each genuinely fits. No single revenue model is universal.

Build in a sensible order

Start useful. Prove demand. Grow responsibly.

The following is a development direction, not a claim that every offer is already available.

01

Publish

Build trust with clear public ideas, useful research and transparent standards.

02

Package

Turn the strongest work into focused guides, publications and practical resources.

03

Serve

Test workshops, research and facilitation against real needs and willingness to pay.

04

Co-create

Develop ventures and shared opportunities with clear agreements and fair value distribution.

First commercial test: choose one specific audience, one costly or frustrating problem, and one small paid deliverable. Validate that people want it before building a large platform, subscription system or marketplace.
Still to be decided

The model should evolve with evidence.

Which audience receives the greatest immediate value from ATOTSM? Which product can be made exceptionally well? What should always remain open? Which collaborations deserve shared ownership? What proportion of revenue should fund research, contributor compensation and new experiments?

These questions should be answered through small tests, transparent decisions and feedback — not by adopting a fashionable business model.

The goal is not to monetise every interaction. It is to build an ecosystem in which valuable work can continue and more people can participate in creating value.
